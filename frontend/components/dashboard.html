Step 1: Polar Coordinate Transformation

Consider the square $I^2 = (\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2} dx)(\int_0^\infty e^{-y^2} dy) = \int_0^\infty \int_0^\infty e^{-(x^2+y^2)} dx dy$.

Step 2: Conversion to First Quadrant Polar Coordinates

Substitute $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$ with Jacobian $dx dy = r dr d\theta$ over $0 \le r < \infty$ and $0 \le \theta \le \pi/2$.

Step 3: Direct Integration & Evaluation

$I^2 = (\pi/2) \int_0^\infty e^{-r^2} r dr = (\pi/2) [-e^{-r^2}/2]_0^\infty = \pi/4 \implies I = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$.
